The story, in order

  1. 2021: Dario and Daniela Amodei and other OpenAI colleagues found Anthropic, a safety-focused AI company.
  2. 2022: An early Claude is trained but held back for more safety testing. The Constitutional AI paper comes out.
  3. March 2023: Claude launches. Claude 2 follows in July.
  4. 2023 onward: Amazon and Google invest billions and supply computing power.
  5. 2024: Claude 3, then Claude 3.5 Sonnet.
  6. 2025: Claude Code and Claude 4. 300,000+ business customers by September.
  7. 2026: The Claude 5 family. Valuation nears $1 trillion.
