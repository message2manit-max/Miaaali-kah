The story, in order

  1. Jan 2020: the "Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models" paper (Kaplan, McCandlish, Amodei and others) at OpenAI.
  2. 2021: Dario and Daniela Amodei, Jared Kaplan, Sam McCandlish, Tom Brown, Chris Olah and Jack Clark found Anthropic. Early meetings happen on Zoom and in San Francisco parks.
  3. Dec 2021: the "helpful, honest, and harmless" framing appears in Anthropic's first big alignment paper.
  4. Summer 2022: an early Claude is trained but held back for more safety testing.
  5. Dec 2022: the Constitutional AI paper.
  6. Mar 14, 2023: Claude is released. Jul 11, 2023: Claude 2 and claude.ai.
  7. Sep 19, 2023: the Responsible Scaling Policy and its AI Safety Levels.
  8. Mar 2024: Claude 3. May 2024: "Scaling Monosemanticity" maps millions of concepts inside Claude, including the Golden Gate Bridge. Jun 2024: Claude 3.5 Sonnet.
  9. Feb 2025: Claude Code preview. May 2025: Claude 4 and Claude Code for everyone.
  10. 2026: the Claude 5 family.
